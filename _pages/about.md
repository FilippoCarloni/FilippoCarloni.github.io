I am a Postdoctoral Researcher at the [National Inter-University Consortium for Telecommunications (CNIT)](https://www.cnit.it/). I am part of the [Networks and Systems group](https://antlab.deib.polimi.it/research-activities/networks-and-systems/) of the Advanced Network Technologies Laboratory (ANTLab) at [Politecnico di Milano](https://www.polimi.it/), under the supervision of [Prof. Gianni Antichi](https://gianniantichi.github.io/).

My research sits at the intersection of **compilers**, **network systems**, and **hardware acceleration**. I am interested in programming abstractions and compilers that let developers express _what_ a network application should do, while the system decides _where_ and _how_ to run it efficiently across heterogeneous network infrastructure, from software to programmable hardware. As part of the EU [FLECON-6G](https://flecon6g.eu/) project, I apply these ideas to offloading network application logic and security functions in next-generation networks.

I started my Ph.D. in Information Technology at Politecnico di Milano in November 2021 and received it in February 2025, with a thesis on [_Exploiting Hardware and Software Specialization for Efficient Regular Expressions Processing_](https://www.politesi.polimi.it/handle/10589/233232), which won the [IEEE Computer Society Italy Chapter PhD Thesis Award](https://site.ieee.org/italy-cs/eventi/) (2025 edition). My Ph.D. work co-designed compilers and domain-specific architectures: an MLIR-based compilation flow ([CGO 2025](https://doi.org/10.1145/3696443.3708916)), automata-level optimizations for multi-pattern matching ([CGO 2024](https://doi.org/10.1109/CGO57630.2024.10444810)), and the ALVEARE compiler/architecture framework ([DAC 2024](https://doi.org/10.1145/3649329.3657378)). During my Ph.D., I was a visiting student at [MIT](https://www.mit.edu/) in the [COMMIT compiler group](https://commit.csail.mit.edu/) led by Prof. Saman Amarasinghe, where I built low-latency, high-throughput SmartNIC processing logic for custom protocols generated by the NetBlocks framework.

The DAC 2024 paper [ALVEARE](https://doi.org/10.1145/3649329.3657378) received the [HiPEAC Paper Award](https://www.hipeac.net/awards/paper/), and I was a Ph.D. Rocca Project Fellow (2024). I am actively involved in [professional service](/service/): I regularly serve on artifact evaluation committees for top systems, architecture, and compiler venues (ASPLOS, EuroSys, PLDI, HPCA, CGO, PPoPP, MICRO), on program committees, and as a reviewer for IEEE and ACM journals, and I received the [Distinguished Artifact Evaluator Award](https://2026.eurosys.org/eurosys-awards.html#awards) at EuroSys 2026.

Since 2022, I have been a Teaching Assistant for [Computer Architecture and Operating Systems](/teaching/) at Politecnico di Milano, a core course of the B.Sc. in Computer Engineering that goes from RISC-V assembly and processor microarchitecture to concurrent programming and operating system internals.

Outside the lab, I am a beekeeper and a truffle hunter: two hobbies that, much like systems research, reward patience and careful observation.

The full list of publications is on the [publications page](/publications/) and on [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=NeORMbQAAAAJ).
