I am a Postdoctoral Researcher at the [National Inter-University Consortium for Telecommunications (CNIT)](https://www.cnit.it/). I am part of the [Networks and Systems group](https://antlab.deib.polimi.it/research-activities/networks-and-systems/) of the Advanced Network Technologies Laboratory (ANTLab) at [Politecnico di Milano](https://www.polimi.it/), where I work with [Prof. Gianni Antichi](https://gianniantichi.github.io/).

My research focuses on **network systems** and **hardware acceleration**. I build frameworks that offload network functions to FPGA- and ASIC-based SmartNICs and DPUs (e.g., NVIDIA BlueField-3), including domain-specific compilers and custom offloading techniques. My current work targets network security applications such as DDoS mitigation, firewalling, and real-time traffic monitoring.

I received my Ph.D. in Information Technology from Politecnico di Milano in 2025, with a thesis on [_Exploiting Hardware and Software Specialization for Efficient Regular Expressions Processing_](https://www.politesi.polimi.it/handle/10589/233232). During my Ph.D., I was a visiting student at [MIT](https://www.mit.edu/) in the [COMMIT group](https://commit.csail.mit.edu/) led by Prof. Saman Amarasinghe, where I worked on low-latency, high-throughput SmartNIC processing logic for custom protocols generated by the NetBlocks framework.

My DAC 2024 paper [ALVEARE](https://doi.org/10.1145/3649329.3657378) received the [HiPEAC Paper Award](https://www.hipeac.net/awards/paper/) and I was a Ph.D. Rocca Project Fellow (2024). I regularly serve on artifact evaluation committees for top systems and architecture venues (ASPLOS, EuroSys, PLDI, HPCA, CGO, PPoPP, MICRO), and received the [Distinguished Artifact Evaluator Award](https://2026.eurosys.org/eurosys-awards.html#awards) at EuroSys 2026.

A full list of my papers is on the [publications page](/publications/) and on [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=NeORMbQAAAAJ).
