Filippo Carloni

Postdoctoral Researcher at CNIT · ANTLab, Politecnico di Milano

ANTLab - Building 20A, Floor 3

20133 Milano, Italy

I am a Postdoctoral Researcher at the National Inter-University Consortium for Telecommunications (CNIT). I am part of the Networks and Systems group of the Advanced Network Technologies Laboratory (ANTLab) at Politecnico di Milano, where I work with Prof. Gianni Antichi.

My research focuses on network systems and hardware acceleration. I build frameworks that offload network functions to FPGA- and ASIC-based SmartNICs and DPUs (e.g., NVIDIA BlueField-3), including domain-specific compilers and custom offloading techniques. My current work targets network security applications such as DDoS mitigation, firewalling, and real-time traffic monitoring.

I received my Ph.D. in Information Technology from Politecnico di Milano in 2025, with a thesis on Exploiting Hardware and Software Specialization for Efficient Regular Expressions Processing. During my Ph.D., I was a visiting student at MIT in the COMMIT group led by Prof. Saman Amarasinghe, where I worked on low-latency, high-throughput SmartNIC processing logic for custom protocols generated by the NetBlocks framework.

My DAC 2024 paper ALVEARE received the HiPEAC Paper Award and I was a Ph.D. Rocca Project Fellow (2024). I regularly serve on artifact evaluation committees for top systems and architecture venues (ASPLOS, EuroSys, PLDI, HPCA, CGO, PPoPP, MICRO), and received the Distinguished Artifact Evaluator Award at EuroSys 2026.

A full list of my papers is on the publications page and on Google Scholar.
